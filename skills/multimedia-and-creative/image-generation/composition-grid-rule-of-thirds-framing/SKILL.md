# Composition Grid Rule Of Thirds Framing

## Overview
AI models tend to place subjects directly in the dead center of the frame. Applying compositional language and aspect ratio anchors shifts visual weight to create dynamic, cinematic balance.

1. **Determine compositional framing structure**:
   - Determine compositional framing structure: rule of thirds off-center, leading lines, or negative space.

2. **Incorporate explicit staging cues into prompt**:
   - Incorporate explicit staging cues into prompt: "subject framed on the left third intersection, vast negative space on right".
