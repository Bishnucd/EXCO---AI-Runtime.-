# Photorealistic Skin Texture Prompt Engineering

## Overview
AI portrait generation often defaults to artificial, plastic-looking airbrushed skin. Engineering prompts with optical camera and biological lighting cues produces natural human skin textures.

## Operational Workflow

1. **Specify authentic skin optical properties**:
   - Specify authentic skin optical properties: "subsurface scattering, delicate skin pores, natural vellus hair".

2. **Include physical camera and lens specifications**:
   - Include physical camera and lens specifications: "captured on 35mm Kodachrome film, 85mm f/1.4 lens, soft rim lighting".

3. **Explicitly eliminate airbrushed plastic keywords**:
   - Explicitly eliminate airbrushed plastic keywords: add "(airbrushed, CGI, plastic, doll, 3D render:1.3)" to negative prompt.

4. **Validate skin specular highlights and pupil reflections under high-zoom inspection.**:
   - Validate skin specular highlights and pupil reflections under high-zoom inspection.
