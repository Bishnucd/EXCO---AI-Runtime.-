# Music Cue Sheet Licensing Breakdown

## Overview
Broadcasters cannot air films without music cue sheets. Cue sheets detail every musical composition, cue duration, usage type (background, theme, visual), and composer royalty splits.

1. **Scan final video timeline to log exact timecode in and timecode out for every piece of music.**:

3. **Specify music usage code**:
   - Specify music usage code: BI (Background Instrumental), BV (Background Vocal), or VV (Visual Vocal).

4. **Export standardized cue sheet Excel/PDF file for distributor delivery and royalty clearance.**:
