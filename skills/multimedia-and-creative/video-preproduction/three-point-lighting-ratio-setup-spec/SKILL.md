# Three Point Lighting Ratio Setup Spec

## Overview
Three-point lighting defines visual mood. Low-contrast corporate interviews use a gentle 1:2 key-to-fill ratio, while film noir and dramatic thrillers use high-contrast 1:8 ratios with deep shadows.

## Operational Workflow

1. **Determine visual aesthetic**:
   - Determine visual aesthetic: High-Key (1:1 to 1:2), Standard Narrative (1:2 to 1:4), or Low-Key Dramatic (1:8+).

2. **Specify Key Light placement**:
   - Specify Key Light placement: 45 degrees off-axis, elevated 45 degrees above subject eye level.

3. **Position Fill Light or passive bounce card opposite Key to control facial shadow density.**:
   - Position Fill Light or passive bounce card opposite Key to control facial shadow density.

4. **Place Hair/Backlight behind subject pointing down to cleanly separate subject shoulders from dark backgrounds.**:
   - Place Hair/Backlight behind subject pointing down to cleanly separate subject shoulders from dark backgrounds.
