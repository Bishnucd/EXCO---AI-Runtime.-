# Location Scout Checklist Audit

## Overview
A visually stunning filming location is useless if airport flight paths ruin audio or insufficient circuit breakers trip under production lights. A location audit prevents disastrous shoot-day surprises.

## Operational Workflow

1. **Assess ambient acoustic environment**:
   - Assess ambient acoustic environment: HVAC noise, traffic rumble, refrigerator hum, and echo reverberation.

2. **Inspect electrical infrastructure**:
   - Inspect electrical infrastructure: circuit breaker capacities, outlet locations, and generator access.

3. **Evaluate natural lighting patterns across morning, afternoon, and evening hours.**:
   - Evaluate natural lighting patterns across morning, afternoon, and evening hours.

4. **Confirm logistical practicalities**:
   - Confirm logistical practicalities: parking permits, crew load-in elevators, and restroom facilities.
