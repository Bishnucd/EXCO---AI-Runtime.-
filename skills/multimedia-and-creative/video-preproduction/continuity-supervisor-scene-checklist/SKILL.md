# Continuity Supervisor Scene Checklist

## Overview
Films are shot out of sequence. A glass half-empty in Take 1 must not be full in Take 2. Script supervisors photograph actor state, log action timing, and mark lined scripts for editors.

## Operational Workflow

1. **Photograph actor wardrobe buttons, hair parting, and prop positions at the start and end of every take.**:
   - Photograph actor wardrobe buttons, hair parting, and prop positions at the start and end of every take.

2. **Log physical action cues**:
   - Log physical action cues: e.g., actor picks up coffee cup on word "Goodbye" with right hand.

3. **Track 180-degree rule and actor eyeline angles across reverse-shot coverage setups.**:
   - Track 180-degree rule and actor eyeline angles across reverse-shot coverage setups.

4. **Produce lined script pages drawing straight lines through covered dialogue and wavy lines for off-camera.**:
   - Produce lined script pages drawing straight lines through covered dialogue and wavy lines for off-camera.
