# Two Column Av Script Authoring

## Overview
Standard screenplay format fails for commercial and documentary videos where visuals and audio run in parallel. A two-column A/V script aligns visual camera directions with narration timecodes.

## Operational Workflow

1. **Structure script into two synchronized columns**:
   - Structure script into two synchronized columns: Left (Visual/Video) and Right (Audio/Sound).

2. **Describe specific visual shots**:
   - Describe specific visual shots: framing (CU, MCU, Wide), graphics, on-screen text, and B-roll.

3. **Draft spoken dialogue and voiceover narration with estimated duration timings.**:
