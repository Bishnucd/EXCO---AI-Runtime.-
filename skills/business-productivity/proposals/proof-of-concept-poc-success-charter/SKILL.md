# Proof Of Concept Poc Success Charter

## Overview
Unstructured POCs drag on indefinitely without closing. A POC success charter establishes a 30-day window, defines 3-5 objective technical pass criteria, and commits to purchase upon success.

## Operational Workflow

1. **Define strict timebox window (typically 14 to 30 calendar days).**:
   - Define strict timebox window (typically 14 to 30 calendar days).

2. **Agree on 3-5 objective, verifiable technical success criteria (e.g. throughput > 5,000 req/s).**:
   - Agree on 3-5 objective, verifiable technical success criteria (e.g. throughput > 5,000 req/s).

3. **Specify customer resource commitments (data access, technical point of contact).**:
   - Specify customer resource commitments (data access, technical point of contact).

4. **Include mutual agreement**:
   - Include mutual agreement: meeting all criteria triggers immediate execution of commercial contract.

## Verification & Execution Criteria
- Pre-execution validation verifies all prerequisites and inputs.
- Step-by-step progress logging confirms intermediate state transitions.
- Post-execution checks verify output integrity and clean exit codes.
