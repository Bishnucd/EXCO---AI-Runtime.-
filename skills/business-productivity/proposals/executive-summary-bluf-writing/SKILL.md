# Executive Summary Bluf Writing

## Overview
Executives rarely read 50-page proposals. A BLUF executive summary delivers the core business challenge, proposed solution, financial ROI, and requested decision in a single compelling page.

## Operational Workflow

1. **State bottom line up front**:
   - State bottom line up front: the exact strategic outcome and investment required.

2. **Quantify the primary business problem and the cost of inaction.**:
   - Quantify the primary business problem and the cost of inaction.

3. **Summarize proposed technical approach and key competitive advantages.**:
   - Summarize proposed technical approach and key competitive advantages.

4. **Present financial summary (investment, payback period, projected NPV) with clear next steps.**:
   - Present financial summary (investment, payback period, projected NPV) with clear next steps.
