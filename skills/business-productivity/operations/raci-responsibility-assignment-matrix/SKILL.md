# Raci Responsibility Assignment Matrix

## Overview
Unclear role ownership causes decision gridlock and missed responsibilities. A RACI matrix ensures every task has exactly one Accountable owner alongside clearly designated Responsible contributors.

## Operational Workflow

1. **Enumerate all critical project milestones, operational decisions, and deliverables.**:
   - Enumerate all critical project milestones, operational decisions, and deliverables.

2. **List participating functional teams, executives, and external partners.**:
   - List participating functional teams, executives, and external partners.

3. **Assign exactly one A (Accountable) and at least one R (Responsible) per activity.**:
   - Assign exactly one A (Accountable) and at least one R (Responsible) per activity.

4. **Verify matrix rules**:
   - Verify matrix rules: no activity with multiple A roles, and no individuals overloaded with R tasks.
