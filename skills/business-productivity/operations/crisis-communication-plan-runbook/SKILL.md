# Crisis Communication Plan Runbook

## Overview
Silence or conflicting statements during major incidents destroy customer trust. A crisis communication playbook establishes designated spokespersons, approval chains, and pre-drafted status templates.

## Operational Workflow

1. **Activate crisis response team and assign designated customer communications lead.**:
   - Activate crisis response team and assign designated customer communications lead.

2. **Post initial "Investigating" update to public status page within 15 minutes of confirmed incident.**:
   - Post initial "Investigating" update to public status page within 15 minutes of confirmed incident.

3. **Select approved holding statements tailored to incident type (outage, data security incident, degraded service).**:
   - Select approved holding statements tailored to incident type (outage, data security incident, degraded service).

4. **Provide regular 30-minute status updates until resolution, followed by formal post-incident customer letters.**:
   - Provide regular 30-minute status updates until resolution, followed by formal post-incident customer letters.
