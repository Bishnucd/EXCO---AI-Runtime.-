# Sean Ellis Pmf Survey Score Analysis

## Overview
The Sean Ellis PMF survey asks users: "How would you feel if you could no longer use this product?" Achieving >40% "Very Disappointed" responses is the recognized benchmark for true Product-Market Fit.

## Operational Workflow

1. **Survey active users who have experienced the core product value at least twice in the past 14 days.**:
   - Survey active users who have experienced the core product value at least twice in the past 14 days.

2. **Analyze responses to primary question**:
   - Analyze responses to primary question: Very Disappointed, Somewhat Disappointed, Not Disappointed.

3. **Calculate PMF percentage score**:
   - Calculate PMF percentage score: (Count of "Very Disappointed" / Total Respondents) * 100.

4. **If score < 40%, pause acquisition spending and interview "Very Disappointed" users to isolate core value.**:
   - If score < 40%, pause acquisition spending and interview "Very Disappointed" users to isolate core value.
