# Product Backlog Refinement De Risking Spikes

## Overview
Uncertain technical requirements cause sprint roll-overs. A research spike is a time-boxed investigation (1–2 days) that writes throwaway code to answer technical unknowns before user story estimation.

## Operational Workflow

1. **Identify backlog user stories with high estimation variance or unknown third-party API dependencies.**:
   - Identify backlog user stories with high estimation variance or unknown third-party API dependencies.

2. **Define clear Spike acceptance criteria**:
   - Define clear Spike acceptance criteria: answer specific questions, test prototype, or establish feasibility.

3. **Time-box research spike to maximum 8–16 engineering hours during preceding sprint.**:
   - Time-box research spike to maximum 8–16 engineering hours during preceding sprint.

4. **Present findings and estimate original user story with high confidence and minimal risk.**:
   - Present findings and estimate original user story with high confidence and minimal risk.
