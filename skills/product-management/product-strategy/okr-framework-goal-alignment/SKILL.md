# Okr Framework Goal Alignment

## Overview
Vague goals produce misaligned engineering effort. Effective OKRs pair ambitious, qualitative Objectives with 3-5 quantitative Key Results measured by leading indicators rather than output checklists.

## Operational Workflow

1. **Draft inspiring, qualitative Objective defining what the team aims to achieve this quarter.**:
   - Draft inspiring, qualitative Objective defining what the team aims to achieve this quarter.

2. **Define 3 to 5 quantitative Key Results with explicit baseline and target metrics.**:
   - Define 3 to 5 quantitative Key Results with explicit baseline and target metrics.

3. **Ensure Key Results measure business outcomes (e.g. conversion lift) rather than shipping outputs (e.g. deploy feature).**:
   - Ensure Key Results measure business outcomes (e.g. conversion lift) rather than shipping outputs (e.g. deploy feature).

4. **Establish bi-weekly confidence scoring (0.0 to 1.0) and retro review cadences.**:
   - Establish bi-weekly confidence scoring (0.0 to 1.0) and retro review cadences.
