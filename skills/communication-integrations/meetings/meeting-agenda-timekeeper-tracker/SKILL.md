# Meeting Agenda Timekeeper Tracker

## Overview
Unstructured meetings frequently run over time without reaching decisions. An agenda timekeeper enforces timeboxed topic slots, tracks speaker time, and logs immediate action decisions.

## Operational Workflow

1. **Define agenda items with explicit time allocations (e.g., 10m update, 20m design debate).**:

2. **Display active countdown timers and time-warning alerts at 5-minute and 1-minute marks.**:

3. **Record formal decisions and consensus agreements under each agenda item in real time.**:
