# Scientific Conflict Of Interest Disclosure Audit

## Overview
Industry-funded studies are significantly more likely to report favorable outcomes. Auditing mandatory ICMJE conflict-of-interest disclosures, patent holdings, and industry consulting fees reveals hidden bias.

## Operational Workflow

1. **Inspect "Competing Interests" and "Funding Acknowledgments" sections in published research paper.**:

2. **Cross-reference author names against Open Payments database (CMS.gov) for pharmaceutical consulting fees.**:
   - Cross-reference author names against Open Payments database (CMS.gov) for pharmaceutical consulting fees.

3. **Search patent databases to identify whether study authors hold commercial patents on the evaluated drug/tech.**:
   - Search patent databases to identify whether study authors hold commercial patents on the evaluated drug/tech.

4. **Assess potential financial bias on experimental design, dosing choices, and comparator drug selections.**:
   - Assess potential financial bias on experimental design, dosing choices, and comparator drug selections.
