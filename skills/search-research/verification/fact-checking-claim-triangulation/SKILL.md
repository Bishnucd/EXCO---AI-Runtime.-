# Fact Checking Claim Triangulation

## Overview
Relying on a single secondary source risks propagating misinformation. Rigorous fact-checking requires triangulating claims across at least two independent primary origin records.

## Operational Workflow

1. **Isolate specific factual claims (dates, numbers, quotes, causal assertions) from narrative context.**:

2. **Trace assertions upstream past secondary aggregators to locate original primary sources.**:

3. **Verify independence**:
   - Verify independence: confirm sources do not merely quote the same wire agency or press release.

4. **Assign verification status (Confirmed, Disputed, Unverified, Debunked) with documented citations.**:
